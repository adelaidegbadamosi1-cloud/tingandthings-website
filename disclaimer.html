AI & Food Disclaimer

Effective date: 28 July 2026

Allergy warning: Never rely solely on Ting & Things or an AI-generated response to identify or exclude allergens.

General information only

Meal plans, recipes, ingredient suggestions and nutritional information are provided for general informational and organisational purposes. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, personalised dietetic advice or emergency guidance.

AI-generated content

Artificial intelligence can misunderstand a request, invent information, omit ingredients or produce incorrect quantities, cooking times and safety advice. Review every output before relying on it.

Allergies and intolerances

Always check the complete packaging label and any “may contain” or cross-contamination warning for every ingredient. Recipes may contain allergens even where a preference or restriction has been entered. Manufacturing formulations change. Contact the manufacturer or a qualified professional where unsure.

Food safety

You are responsible for safe shopping, storage, preparation and cooking. Follow current guidance from relevant food-safety authorities and product packaging. Check that food is thoroughly cooked and safe for the person eating it. Do not rely solely on a visual description, estimated time or AI-generated temperature.

Nutrition

Nutritional values, calories, portion sizes and cost estimates may be approximate and can vary by brand, ingredient, preparation and serving size. Consult a registered healthcare or nutrition professional before making significant dietary changes, particularly for pregnancy, children, older adults, medical conditions, eating disorders or prescribed diets.

Emergency

For a suspected severe allergic reaction or other emergency, contact emergency services immediately. Ting & Things is not monitored for emergencies.

Your responsibility

By using the app, you accept responsibility for checking whether suggestions are suitable for you and anyone you cook for. Stop using a suggestion where you have any doubt about its safety.
