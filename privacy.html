5. AI processing

Information included in a meal-planning request may be sent to AI services used to generate the requested output. Ting & Things currently uses Rork as part of its application platform and OpenAI for AI-enabled functionality. Do not enter confidential information that is unnecessary for your request. Avoid including another person's personal information without permission.

AI outputs can be inaccurate. Please read our AI & Food Disclaimer.
