Support

Need help with Ting & Things? Email us and include the email address connected to your account, your device type and a brief description of the issue. Do not send passwords or full payment-card details.

Email

tingandthingsapp@gmail.com

Typical response target: 2–3 UK business days.

Common requests

  • Billing or refunds: Apple or Google usually handles these through the store used for purchase.
  • Restore a purchase: Use “Restore Purchases” in the app where available.
  • Cancel a subscription: Cancel through App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Delete your account: See Delete Your Account.
  • Privacy request: Email us with “Privacy request” in the subject.
